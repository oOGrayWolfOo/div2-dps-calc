How that number was built

Enter your stats to see the breakdown.

Assumptions, stated plainly: crit and headshot bonuses ADD inside one bracket — a crit headshot is damage × (1 + Headshot Damage + Crit Damage), confirmed against shooting-range data on Oct 11, 2026 (the separate-factors reading was tested and rejected). The gear-set multiplier sits in its own factor after Total Weapon Damage (per the kit explainer); amplified effects each multiply on their own; a talent's uptime scales its percent for expected-value math. Weapon Damage is an at-rest value — conditional talents at stacks are added here, never double-counted.

Filter boxes narrow each table as you type. Export buttons download the visible (filtered) rows of that table as CSV or plain text. Bucket key: WD = additive weapon damage pool, TWD = total weapon damage (own multiplier), AMP = amplified (own multiplier each), GSM = gear set multiplier, CHD = crit damage, HSD = headshot damage, DTA/DTH/DTTOC = armor / health / out of cover.
